Quick Strategy

The 12 rules that cover most hands. Learn these first.

Dealer shows 2–6

They're weakLet them bust. Stand on stiff hands.

Dealer shows 7–A

They're strongImprove your hand. Take a card.

Splits
  • PAlways split Aces and 8s — every time.
  • HNever split 5s or 10s — you're breaking a strong hand.
  • PSplit the other pairs only when the dealer is weak (2–6).
Hard Hands
  • H8 or less → always hit.
  • D10 or 11 → double, unless the dealer shows 10 or Ace.
  • S12–16 → stand vs 2–6, hit vs 7–A. (But hit 12 vs 2&3.)
  • S17 or more → always stand.
Soft Hands · Ace counts as 11
  • HSoft 17 or less → hit (double vs 5&6).
  • SSoft 18 → stand vs 2–8, hit vs 9–A.
  • SSoft 19 or more → always stand.
Golden rule: assume the dealer's hidden card is a 10.
